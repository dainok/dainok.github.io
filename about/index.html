      About

      LinkedIn | GitHub | Podcast | Email

      Andrea Dainese works alongside organizations as a cybersecurity advisor with over 20 years of experience protecting critical infrastructure and industrial control systems (ICS/OT). He helps organizations define a concrete and effective security strategy, aligned with international standards, leveraging automation to scale compliance, defenses, and optimize operational processes.

      Known in the technical community as the father of UNetLab (now EVE-NG), he is an author, podcast host, and active mentor for professionals looking to grow into CISO-level roles.

      He also brings digital security awareness into schools, educating students and adults on how to recognize and defend against cyber threats.

      For Andrea, hacking means understanding something deeply and making it better, and that includes oneself. After years of growing more sensitive to the cold, he decided to stop avoiding it and train for it instead. Cold-water immersion and daily cold showers became a practice in consistency and self-discipline, and a reminder that discomfort fades if you stay with it. He brings the same mindset to his work: understand the system, respect the process, and keep going.
