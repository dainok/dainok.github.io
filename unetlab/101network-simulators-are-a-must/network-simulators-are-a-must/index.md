For anyone working in IT, theory alone is not enough. Practical experience is crucial to grasp concepts and develop the skills needed to design, configure and manage complex environments. In the early 2000s, network administrators often bought second-hand hardware to build labs where they could study and experiment with configurations before deploying them in production.

Then virtualization arrived. It simplified the process and made labs accessible to many people without expensive hardware. Network administrators used simulators first, but they have since become foundational for the training of all IT professionals.

Today, it is unimaginable to work without simulated environments. Both professionals and students need them to experiment, study and stay up to date. Simulators were once mainly for network administrators, but now they include ready-made scenarios for both defensive and offensive security.

## The Benefits of Using Network Simulators

* **Accessibility:** from students to professionals, anyone can access complex network environments without expensive equipment.
* **Safety:** we can run tests in a secure environment, without the risk of compromising real networks.
* **Flexibility:** we can experiment with configurations that would be impractical or too risky on a real network.
* **Certification preparation:** for those preparing for certifications like CCNA, CCNP and others, these tools provide a practical environment to practice.
* **Demos and PoCs:** a simulated environment is ideal for showing technological solutions realistically.

## Conclusions

Network simulators and emulators are now indispensable in IT training and research. They offer a safe, accessible and flexible way to experiment and learn, so we can acquire practical skills without the constraints of physical hardware. Whether you are a student aiming for a certification or a professional who wants to test new configurations, these tools are an essential part of your toolkit.
