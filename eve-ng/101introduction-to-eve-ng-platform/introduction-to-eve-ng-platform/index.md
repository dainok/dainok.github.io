Developing automations that interact with network devices requires a development environment to test, learn and experiment with frameworks, integrations and automations. Over the years, virtualization has given anyone realistic copies of network devices. Today, anyone can, and should, have their own development environment.

In the past, I developed a web-based application for creating and managing virtual network labs: [UNetLab](https://www.adainese.it/blog/2019/01/01/unified-networking-lab-unetlab-the-story/), now managed by third parties under the name [EVE-NG](https://www.eve-ng.net/). It lets us implement our testing environment.

The EVE-NG Workbook is the guide for the students of my network automation courses, so that they can build an independent lab where they can study and develop automations and integrations.

If you find these guides useful, you can support the work on Patreon to get more in-depth guides and tutorials.
