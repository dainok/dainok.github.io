This series is about **Ansible**, a tool that automates a wide range of IT tasks, from configuration management to application deployment, and simplifies the orchestration of complex infrastructures.

We start from the basics and move gradually to advanced topics, with hands-on examples, best practices and practical advice. The series is for those who are new to automation and for those who want to improve their existing skills.
