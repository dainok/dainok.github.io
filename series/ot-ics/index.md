## Visibility, Observability, and Segregation in OT/ICS

Visibility and observability are critical to secure and manage industrial OT/ICS environments. Visibility means identifying all the assets, communications and network activities. Observability gives deeper insight into system behavior, anomalies and potential threats. Segregating OT/ICS networks with VLANs, firewalls and air-gapping reduces the attack surface by isolating critical systems from IT and external networks. This layered approach improves security, reduces operational risk and supports compliance with industry regulations.
