This series collects what I learned in the ISA IC32 course on IEC 62443, the standard series for the cybersecurity of industrial automation and control systems. It has three goals: to give a big picture of the standard, to turn it into a strategy that we can implement step by step, and to help prepare for the exam.

We start with the map: why OT security is different from IT, how the standard is organized, who is responsible for what, zones and conduits, security levels, and the models and lifecycles that tie everything together. Then we follow the lifecycle from the first decision to the last patch. In this practical part, every post ends with something concrete that we can produce.

No prior knowledge of the standard is needed, only some curiosity about how plants are kept secure without stopping them.
